- ✔ **npc-acts-unprompted** (turn 2): Multiple NPCs acted without direct player input: Sister Maren and the pilgrim showed dynamic reactions (e.g., shifting posture, dialogue changes) in turns 2-16. The Ash Ghoul and Ashen Revenant also moved and attacked autonomously in turns 21-29.
- ✔ **recap-of-world-moves** (turn 42): The session summary at turn 42 listed world actions: opportunities offered/expired and a mood transition in the Crypt Depths.
